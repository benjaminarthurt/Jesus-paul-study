# Succession and council recheck

## irenaeus-succession-reception

Against Heresies III.3 §§1–4, Roberts and Rambaut, ANF 1 (1885), New Advent. Explicit attribution to Paul of Titus 3:10–11 in §4 and reference to Timothy regarding Linus in §3. These demonstrate reception of disputed Pauline letters, not historical verification of succession lists. https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103303.htm

## nicaea-doctrine-governance

New Advent English text, creed and canons 2–5, 8–11 rechecked. Canon 2 explicitly quotes the novice restriction of 1 Timothy 3:6; its wording also resembles 3:7. Canon 5 requires review of exclusions; canon 8 conditions reintegration on communion with twice-married and repentant persons. Canon 11 specifies staged penance. Additional candidates: canon 17 invokes Psalm 15 against clerical usury, canon 19 concerns specifically Paulianist deaconesses, not every female minister. Edition details and original texts pending. https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm

All remain draft. No causal inference or original-language verification claimed.
