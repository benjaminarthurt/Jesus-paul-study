# Historical reception research plan

Use dated primary documents and precise section locators. Distinguish direct quotation, probable allusion, explicit doctrinal interpretation, institutional adoption and modern application. Do not infer causal influence merely from resemblance.

## Workstreams

1. First and second centuries: Clement, Ignatius, Didache, Justin, Irenaeus; institutional authority, Torah, charity and discipline.
2. Late antiquity: Augustine, Chrysostom, councils; grace, coercion, wealth, marriage.
3. Medieval reception: representative Eastern and Western theologians, canon law and institutional policies.
4. Reformation: Lutheran, Reformed, Catholic and radical reform texts; justification, civil authority and church order.
5. Modern reception: denominational documents and contrasting interpretations of slavery, gender, public welfare and war.

For each record document date, genre, textual references, argument, evidence of influence, counterexamples, source access and unresolved questions. Avoid treating any author as representative of every subsequent tradition.

Initial study: Augustine on grace and works (research/reviews/augustine-grace-reception.md). Primary excerpts inspected; later institutional reception not yet verified.
