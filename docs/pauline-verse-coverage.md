# Pauline verse coverage audit

The provisional chapter-end verse numbers follow conventional English New Testament numbering. They are manually entered and have not yet been independently reconciled against WEBP. Numbered slots are not necessarily extant verses in every edition. The audit counts unique numbered slots intersecting indexed references, including cross-chapter spans, not verified teachings. Overlapping references do not inflate counts. Missing slots are candidates for extraction or documented exclusion; greetings and narrative material are not automatically teachings.

The dashboard computes gaps at build time. No research status is promoted by this audit.
