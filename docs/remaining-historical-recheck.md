# Remaining historical primary-source audit

Date: 2026-11-26. Selected English text retrieved and inspected, not complete works or independent scholarship. Scope is limited to the passages below. Schleitheim translation: William R. Estep Jr., ed., Anabaptist Beginnings (1976), pp. 100–105, as credited by GHDI. Other edition metadata remains to be consolidated.

## clement-corinth-reception

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1010.htm) — Chs 1–2, 5, 7–8, 46–49.

Chapter 47 expressly invokes Paul's Corinthian letter against faction, and chapter 46 places Jesus' warning about stumbling blocks alongside Pauline body language. Chapter 8 connects repentance with Isaiah's demands for justice. Distinguish the author's wording from editorial verse links.

## justin-covenant-reception

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/01282.htm) — Chs 10–11.

Trypho challenges failure to observe circumcision and Sabbath; Justin presents Christ as the universal successor covenant. This is a literary dialogue; similarity to Paul does not establish direct borrowing or represent all Judaism.

## tertullian-doctrinal-boundaries

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0311.htm) — Chs 1, 3–4.

Combines Matthew 7:15 with 1 Corinthians 11:19 and disputes an interpretation of 1 Thessalonians 5:21. Explicit reception of both traditions; not proof that dissenters actually held the views attributed to them.

## chrysostom-baptism-ethics

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/210211.htm) — Opening exposition of Romans 6:5.

Distinguishes baptismal forgiveness as gift from ongoing moral effort supported by God; identifies doctrinal and ethical exhortation as interwoven in Romans.

## augustine-coercion-reception

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1102093.htm) — Chapter 1 §§1–3; chapter 2 §§4–5.

Uses Matthew 5:45, Luke 14:23 and Saul's conversion in Acts to defend corrective coercion. The latter is narrative reception, not a command from Paul's letters. His reported effectiveness is not independent evidence.

## augustine-grace-reception

[English source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1510.htm) — Retractions extract and chapters 1–2 opening.

Presents grace and free choice as compatible, invokes Philippians 3:12–16 and argues that moral commands presuppose agency. Later chapters and companion works still require collation.

## augsburg-justification-reception

[English source](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/of-justification/) — Article IV §§1–3.

Justification through faith rather than human merit; Romans 3–4 explicitly referenced. Article VI on obedience remains complementary context.

## augsburg-civil-authority-reception

[English source](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/of-civil-affairs/) — Article XVI §§1–7.

Permits civil offices, just war and required oaths; obedience bounded by Acts 5:29. Romans 13 is a comparison, not cited explicitly in this article.

## trent-justification-reception

[English source](https://adfonteslibrary.org/work-council-trent-session-06) — Session VI chapters VII–XI.

Initial justification is unmerited, while renewal and growth involve grace-enabled conduct. Chapter VII combines James 2, Galatians 5:6 and Matthew 19:17; chapter XI invokes 1 Corinthians 9. Canons and edition identification remain pending.

## schleitheim-baptism

[English source](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/an-anabaptist-confession-of-faith-the-schleitheim-articles-1527) — Article I.

Repentant voluntary recipients; burial and resurrection language resembles Romans 6, while explicit references list Matthew, Mark and Acts. Mark 16 textual status requires caution.

## schleitheim-discipline

[English source](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/an-anabaptist-confession-of-faith-the-schleitheim-articles-1527) — Articles II–III.

Private admonitions before public discipline, citing Matthew 18; communion boundaries invoke Pauline table imagery. Separate textual status from practical implementation.

## schleitheim-pastors

[English source](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/an-anabaptist-confession-of-faith-the-schleitheim-articles-1527) — Article V.

Pastoral qualifications, congregational support and public accountability use Pauline material including traditional Pastorals. Authorship remains distinguished from confessional attribution.

## schleitheim-sword

[English source](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/an-anabaptist-confession-of-faith-the-schleitheim-articles-1527) — Article VI.

Rejects Christian participation in coercive magistracy while accepting secular government has a role; appeals to Jesus and Pauline conformity and spiritual warfare. Uses the disputed adulterous-woman passage.

## schleitheim-oaths

[English source](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/an-anabaptist-confession-of-faith-the-schleitheim-articles-1527) — Article VII.

Rejects oaths and expressly answers objections based on Peter and Paul by distinguishing testimony from promises; compare but do not assume this resolves Galatians 1:20 or 2 Corinthians 1:23.

## catechism-war

[English source](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_two/article_5/iii_safeguarding_peace.html) — §§2302–2317.

Affirms enemy love and nonviolent witness, conditional military self-defense, conscience accommodation and limits on wartime actions. Distinguish explicit Gospel citations from thematic comparison with Romans 13.

## joint-declaration-law

[English source](https://www.christianunity.va/content/unitacristiani/en/dialoghi/sezione-occidentale/luterani/dialogo/documenti-di-dialogo/1999-dichiarazione-congiunta-sulla-dottrina-della-giustificazion/en.html) — §§26–31.

Paragraph 31 joins Romans 3:28 with continuing validity of commandments and Jesus' example. Page metadata identifies 1997 document; final signing texts not yet checked.
