# Biblical text, licensing, and reuse policy

Until translation and rights review is complete, the website displays **references and original paraphrases only**.

Potential future primary display text: a public-domain English Bible such as the World English Bible, subject to checking the publisher's copyright/trademark notice and exact edition. Modern copyrighted translations require their own rights assessment and quotation limits.

Greek/Hebrew text editions, critical apparatus, modern commentaries, and scholarly articles may have separate copyrights. Do not assume scholarly use permits unrestricted republication.

**Repository licensing:** no LICENSE file is included yet. Do not imply open-source or Creative Commons permissions for original code and writing until a deliberate licensing decision is recorded. Attribution and quotation rights for external sources must be handled separately.
