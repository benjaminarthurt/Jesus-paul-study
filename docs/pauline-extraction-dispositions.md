# Pauline extraction dispositions

The contextual pass covered the 42 context-priority parents and three cross-chapter parents inherited from PR #88. Each inspected passage has a WEBP edition, date, chapter URLs and an editorial note. The 75 new verse units distinguish literary form from the voice credited with the words. Each unit stays inside one chapter so citations can point to the correct source page. A cross-chapter parent holds the argument together.

A context-only disposition retains the passage and explains its use. A greeting can establish who hosted an assembly without prescribing who may hold office. Mixed passages retain prayers, claims and instructions alongside notes on personal names, travel and other contextual material. Existing historical and academic review states remain separate from this English-text inspection. That initial pass brought the catalog to 436 draft units. A subsequent six-chapter pass revisited 35 existing candidates and added 24. Each of its 17 parents now records the extent of English inspection. The attribution review also identifies the limited Greek wording and scholarly excerpts consulted.

The Gospel extraction queue also records passage-level English checks. Its units distinguish direct words, reported words and narrated actions. Verse endpoints locate a unit; notes identify changes of speaker inside a verse. Disputed material is displayed with its parent even when excluded from the core sayings catalog. An undirected pair in gospel-audit-links.json appears at both endpoints. Each relationship identifies its scope and the remaining differences.
