## English inspection across thirteen letters

All 218 indexed Pauline parents carry an English edition, a chapter link, an inspection date and a passage-specific disposition. The catalog contains 542 draft units. The final 142 parents contributed 80 additional units, including the building and temple images in 1 Corinthians 3, the collection delegates in 2 Corinthians 8 and the petitions in Romans 15. The narrower parent for 1 Corinthians 8:4–6 supplies the records for those verses within the wider argument.

Literary form describes a unit's main function. Attribution identifies the voice it presents. A prayer can be the letter writer's speech, a received saying has a transmission claim, and an exhortation can contain a quotation whose boundaries are disputed. A greeting can identify a distinct speaker, as with Tertius in Romans 16:22. The final doxology in Romans retains its conventional 16:25–27 reference. The linked WEBP chapter supplies a placement note and prints the words in 14:24–26.

The English pass does not establish exhaustive clause coverage, the priority of any manuscript, or agreement about authorship. The six disputed letters retain their authorship labels. Greek collation, comparison of variant witnesses, and independent scholarly review remain open.
