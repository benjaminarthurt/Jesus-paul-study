# Early reception recheck, 2026-11-26

Selected English translations inspected directly on New Advent. Original languages and independent scholarship remain pending.

## didache-ethical-reception

Source: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0714.htm

Chapters 1–6: generosity qualified by recipient need and discernment; household slave obedience alongside common divine authority; differentiated obligations and caution on idol food. Ephesians and Colossians links are editorial cross-references, not explicit citations.

## ignatius-authority-reception

Source: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0105.htm

Chapters 2–10: bishop, presbyters and deacons; obedience connected with divine authority; chapter 9 English rendering explicitly mentions Lord's Day, Greek interpretation pending. Chapter 10 polemic should not be endorsed.

## ignatius-unity-reception

Source: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0108.htm

Chapters 1–9: bishop's meekness; repentance and unity in chapters 3 and 8; single Eucharist in 4; divine witness in 7. Galatians 1:1 link is editorial; verbal resemblance is not proven dependence.
