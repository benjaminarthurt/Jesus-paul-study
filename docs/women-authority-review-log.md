- Armin D. Baum (2014), *Paul's Conflicting Statements on Female Public Speaking*, *Tyndale Bulletin*: **publisher bibliographic summary only**; fuller proposal not inspected.

## Editorial guardrails
1. Distinguish Gospel witness and teaching from reported acts and from later office definitions.
2. Do not replace Mark 16's shorter ending with the disputed 16:9–20 as though its status were uncontroversial.
3. Treat 1 Corinthians as generally undisputed Pauline authorship but 14:34–35 as a disputed *passage within that letter*.
4. Mark *transposition* after verse 40 as different from an extant *omission*. Never say surviving manuscripts universally lack the verses.
5. Treat 1 Timothy and Titus as disputed Pauline witnesses, not as if their author were identified with certainty.
6. Opposing interpretations of Junia's status and Greek terminology must be presented with accurate access-scope labels.
7. Church order, ordination and civil equality are distinct modern questions.
