# John literary/sayings catalog — initial review log

**November 18, 2026. Status: draft.** This is a structural/content iteration, **not** completed historical or Greek-text research.

## Sources inspected
- [World English Bible, Protestant edition (WEBP), John 5](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JHN05.htm), [John 7](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JHN07.htm), and [John 8](https://ebible.org/engwebp/JHN08.htm): accessible chapter text and verse numbering; **not** a manual verse-by-verse full-Gospel audit.

## Decisions
- Preserve existing John 4:7–42 and John 20:11–18 anchors from the women's authority pilot.
- A 21-chapter, 88-unit index covers every reference verse once; units are editorial and do not settle historicity or the earliest text.
- Add 93 draft, selected speech-bearing spans; these are paraphrases and not full quotations.
- Restrict candidate parallels: many of John's distinctive discourses lack directly comparable Synoptic contexts.
- Explicitly **exclude** contested John 7:53–8:11 from the core Jesus-sayings registry; preserve both half-chapter reference records for navigating the traditional placement.
- Mark John 3's speaker-boundary ambiguities as editorial caution **without** turning them into the manuscript-variant search facet.
- Mark interpretive debates over John 20:23 and John 21 as cautions, not unverified manuscript variants.
- Keep all existing Paul/Acts source-layer distinctions and comparison evidence records unchanged.

## John 1–7 passage inspection

Each indexed parent through chapter 7 now has a WEBP chapter link and a passage-specific disposition. In John 1 the Baptist reports another speaker's instructions, while Philip gives his own invitation. In John 4 the woman retells the encounter and the townspeople speak for themselves. In 5:18 and 7:39 narrator explanations accompany recorded words. In 6:64 the change of voice falls inside a verse. These distinctions are recorded at the passage level, including where the earlier speech catalog groups multiple speakers within one span.

Five reciprocal comparison links distinguish the feeding, water crossing and temple action from a proverb reused in another setting and a thematic resemblance between call narratives. The records give English evidence for the scope of the relationship. Literary dependence, Greek wording, textual variants and independent scholarly assessment remain open.

All 88 indexed parents of John now have WEBP notes, including the remaining 47 in chapters 8–21. The audit distinguishes the healed man's argument in 9:31, Martha's confession in 11:27, the narrator's fulfillment reference in 18:9 and Jesus' own replies at the hearing. The Johannine inventory contains 204 verse-bounded units of speech and action. Seven additional links compare the anointing, entry, denial prediction, arrest, hearing, death and burial with Mark. Readers can cite a unit directly using its linked verse range. Manuscript adjudication and independent review remain pending.
