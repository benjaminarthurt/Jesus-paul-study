# Luke Gospel catalog: research review log

**November 18, 2026 — preliminary catalog, not independently reviewed.**

## Sources consulted
- World English Bible Protestant 66-book edition; chapter URLs rooted at `https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUKXX.htm`. The site links original chapter text externally; research summaries are not copied translations.
- [Yale Bible Study: Introduction to the Gospel of Luke](https://yalebiblestudy.org/courses/the-gospel-of-luke/lessons/introduction-to-the-course-4/) — publicly accessible full introduction with a literary structure and discussion of Luke's distinctive themes. This is a secondary overview, not a critical Greek text.
- Dirk Jongkind, [Luke 22:43–44](https://tyndalehouse.com/2018/11/15/luke-2243-44-is-the-angel-and-the-sweat-like-drops-of-blood-an-early-addition/) — accessible Tyndale House essay comparing manuscript support, text-critical theories and edition decisions. It endorses inclusion with uncertainty.
- Dirk Jongkind, [Luke 23:34a](https://tyndalehouse.com/2018/11/15/father-forgive-them-the-variant-in-luke-2334a/) — accessible Tyndale House essay arguing for inclusion while acknowledging important witnesses omitting the phrase.

## Deliberate guardrails
- 24-chapter arithmetic coverage is a **reference-number integrity test**, not an assertion of exact pericope division.
- Ten preexisting Luke research anchors retain their stable IDs, source layers and scope.
- Speech-bearing records have parent literary units and retain their narrated audiences, but some spans may still include other speakers.
- Parallels remain `candidate` with `parallel` or `related` statuses; the site does not assume word-for-word identity or independent attestation.
- Distinctive Luke stories remain distinct. Luke 1's Mary's/Zechariah's songs are not indexed as utterances by Jesus.
- Variants are not resolved by borrowing a preferred translation, text-critical blog or one verse's later popularity.
- All new records remain `draft`.
