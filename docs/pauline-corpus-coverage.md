# Pauline corpus coverage

All 87 chapters of the thirteen traditionally attributed letters have a preliminary chapter-level inventory in `research/index/pauline-chapters.json`. This is a navigation and triage layer, not exhaustive passage extraction. Chapter summaries are draft editorial descriptions, not quotations or verified critical-edition findings.

Each chapter retains its authorship category, primary source chapter URL and a provisional primary theme. Existing passage IDs remain unchanged. Next, subdivide every chapter into teaching units, document non-teaching material and check verse coverage against a fixed edition. Chapter coverage must never be reported as complete teaching coverage.
