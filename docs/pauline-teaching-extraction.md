# Pauline teaching extraction

Initial granular candidate inventory across all thirteen traditionally attributed letters. Entries identify selected teaching ranges, not exhaustive coverage or verbatim quotations. Authorship layers are inherited from parent passages; two explicitly attributed Jesus traditions are distinguished from letter discourse. Titles are editorial paraphrases from preliminary knowledge, not fresh source collation. Overlapping parent units are resolved to the narrowest containing unit.
