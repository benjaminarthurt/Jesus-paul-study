# Research and website roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (this PR)

- Project charter, corpus separation, editorial standards, licensing policy.
- Stable 27-book registry and illustrative passage registry.
- Astro static website with topics, book browsing, modern-issue pages and comparison views.
- Automated referential-integrity checks.

## Phase 2 — Gospels (underway)

The initial Mark map adds 77 draft literary units across all 16 chapters, with source chapter links and distinctions among speech, dialogue, action, and contextual narrative. This is **not** a verified exhaustive index of sayings. Next passes must map precise speeches, textual variants, Synoptic parallels and scholarly sources.

- Exhaustive pericope inventory and Jesus-speech/action tags for Mark, Matthew, Luke, John.
- Synoptic parallel map; Gospel-specific differences; significant variants.

## Phase 3 — Paul and early Christianity

- Seven undisputed letters; six debated letters separately.
- Acts versus Paul's autobiographical testimony.
- Remaining New Testament and relevant Hebrew Bible context.

## Phase 4 — Three pilot studies

1. Law: Matthew 5 versus Galatians 3 and Romans.
2. Wealth: Synoptic warnings, Pauline collections and generosity.
3. Women: Gospel participation, Romans 16, 1 Corinthians 14, 1 Timothy 2.

The initial Markdown pages are outlines only; research is **not yet complete**.

## Phase 5 — Modern-issue dossiers

Immigration, poverty, institutional power, gender, sexual ethics, war, punishment, labor, healthcare, religious liberty, and other issues. Always distinguish direct text from inferred policy application.

## Phase 6 — Publish

Search, source bibliography, parallel text viewer, textual-variant callouts, clear review badges, accessibility audits, stable public URLs, and deployment.

## Evidence-ledger pilot (November 2026)

The Law and Faith comparison links contextual passages, six scholarly/primary source records with declared access scopes, and six claim-level entries with counterarguments and limitations. The website now provides a searchable research foundation and a bibliography catalog, but **all research conclusions remain draft** pending detailed language, textual-variant and independent editorial checks. See `docs/mosaic-law-review-log.md`.

## Wealth and poverty comparative pilot (November 2026)

A new comparative study covers Luke's blessings, the rich ruler, Matthew 25, Paul's cross-community collection, Zacchaeus, Mark's widow, and Acts/1 Timothy as **separate contextual voices**. It provides source-aware counterreadings and competing interpretations in a claim-level ledger. **Draft only**: a deeper textual and scholarly review remains. See `docs/wealth-poverty-review-log.md`.

## Women and authority pilot (November 2026)

The third evidence-ledger study now differentiates Gospel witnesses, Romans 16 coworkers, 1 Corinthians 11 and 14, Galatians 3:28, and Acts/Pastorals as separate witness groups. It includes scholarly disputes over Junia, silence in worship, manuscript transposition, and the Pastorals' authorship. All records remain **draft** pending critical-text audit and independent review. See `docs/women-authority-review-log.md`.

## Phase-two reference tools (November 2026)

Issue #6 now has a static-first indexed-passage search and two-pane source-linked reference viewer. The UI exposes review-state and attribution labels, shared pair URLs, and a stable per-passage citation route; it does **not** embed full Bible text or claim independent manuscript verification. Canonical absolute URLs activate only once a public deployment origin is configured. See `docs/search-and-reader.md`. Manual accessibility QA and deployment checks remain open.

## Matthew literary and speech-bearing inventory (November 2026)

The first Matthew index assigns all verses in Matthew's 28 chapters to 119 preliminary literary units, including the eight previously indexed study anchors. The draft speech layer adds 75 selected spans with 94 cross-Gospel **candidate** references. The browser now supports Mark and Matthew with book-specific filters, links and textual cautions. The new bounds and parent-link tests establish structural integrity, not final manuscript or historical verification. See `research/books/matthew.md` and `docs/matthew-review-log.md`.

## Luke literary and sayings catalog (November 2026)

The next Gospel pilot maps Luke's 24 chapters into 143 preliminary literary units (including existing research anchors), adds 112 selected speech-bearing spans and 113 cross-Gospel candidate relationships, and makes Luke selectable in book, teaching and search views. It maintains textual warnings for Luke 10's envoy-number variant and disputed readings in the Passion accounts. All material remains **draft**, pending verse and Greek text review, detailed comparison of parallel passages, and independent editing. See `research/books/luke.md` and `docs/luke-review-log.md`.

## John literary and speech-bearing catalog (November 2026)

The John pilot maps all 21 chapters into 88 draft literary units (including two preserved anchor passages), adds 93 selected speech-bearing spans and 15 conservative cross-Gospel candidate links, and extends navigation to all four Gospels. A validation guard excludes the textually contested John 7:53–8:11 from the core sayings layer. John 3 narrator/Jesus speech boundary ambiguity is separated from true manuscript variants in the search facet. Manuscript cautions cover John 1:18, 5:3b–4 and 9:35, with interpretive cautions for John 20 and the epilogue. See `research/books/john.md` and `docs/john-review-log.md`. **Draft, subject to independent scholarly checking.**
