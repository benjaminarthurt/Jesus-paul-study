# Research and website roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (this PR)

- Project charter, corpus separation, editorial standards, licensing policy.
- Stable 27-book registry and illustrative passage registry.
- Astro static website with topics, book browsing, modern-issue pages and comparison views.
- Automated referential-integrity checks.

## Phase 2 — Gospels (underway)

The initial Mark map adds 77 draft literary units across all 16 chapters, with source chapter links and distinctions among speech, dialogue, action, and contextual narrative. This is **not** a verified exhaustive index of sayings. Next passes must map precise speeches, textual variants, Synoptic parallels and scholarly sources.

- Exhaustive pericope inventory and Jesus-speech/action tags for Mark, Matthew, Luke, John.
- Synoptic parallel map; Gospel-specific differences; significant variants.

## Phase 3 — Paul and early Christianity

- Seven undisputed letters; six debated letters separately.
- Acts versus Paul's autobiographical testimony.
- Remaining New Testament and relevant Hebrew Bible context.

## Phase 4 — Three pilot studies

1. Law: Matthew 5 versus Galatians 3 and Romans.
2. Wealth: Synoptic warnings, Pauline collections and generosity.
3. Women: Gospel participation, Romans 16, 1 Corinthians 14, 1 Timothy 2.

The initial Markdown pages are outlines only; research is **not yet complete**.

## Phase 5 — Modern-issue dossiers

Immigration, poverty, institutional power, gender, sexual ethics, war, punishment, labor, healthcare, religious liberty, and other issues. Always distinguish direct text from inferred policy application.

## Phase 6 — Publish

Search, source bibliography, parallel text viewer, textual-variant callouts, clear review badges, accessibility audits, stable public URLs, and deployment.

## Evidence-ledger pilot (November 2026)

The Law and Faith comparison links contextual passages, six scholarly/primary source records with declared access scopes, and six claim-level entries with counterarguments and limitations. The website now provides a searchable research foundation and a bibliography catalog, but **all research conclusions remain draft** pending detailed language, textual-variant and independent editorial checks. See `docs/mosaic-law-review-log.md`.
