# Aquinas translation recheck

2026-11-26: selected English text retrieved from New Advent. Latin and independent scholarship pending.

## aquinas-war-reception

II-II q40 a1, https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3040.htm, objections 1–2, answer and replies 1–3. Romans 13:4 explicitly extended from domestic punishment to external defense; Matthew 5:39 and Romans 12:19 treated together in reply 2. Augustine quotation in answer has editorial attribution correction; trace separately.

## aquinas-property-reception

II-II q66 aa1–2, https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3066.htm. Article 1 reply 2 criticizes absolute ownership in Luke 12:18; article 2 answer explicitly cites 1 Timothy 6:17–18 for sharing. Distinguishes administration and use; ownership arises from human agreement in reply 1.

## aquinas-lending-reception

II-II q78 a1, https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3078.htm, objections and answer, replies 1–4. Luke 19:23 spiritual interpretation, Luke 6:35 differentiated lending duty and profit prohibition; civil permission does not establish moral approval. Anti-Jewish stereotype in reply 2 is author's prejudice, not historical fact.
