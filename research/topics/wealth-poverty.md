## Research question

Do Jesus and Paul envision the same obligations toward wealth, possessions, and people in need?

## Initial observations

Luke's blessings and woes, the rich man narrative, Matthew's judgment scene, and Paul's collection for believers must each be read in context. The objects of aid and the demands described are not always identical.

## Questions to investigate

- Is Jesus' demand to relinquish possessions specific to particular encounters or a general ethic?
- Does Paul's collection reflect continuity, modification, or a different problem?
- What changes when modern prosperity teaching or economic policy is brought into the discussion?
