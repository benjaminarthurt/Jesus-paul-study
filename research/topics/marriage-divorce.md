## Research question

Does Paul change Jesus' instructions about divorce or apply them to a situation Jesus is not recorded addressing?

Paul explicitly distinguishes an instruction he attributes to the Lord from further guidance. The relation between those two parts must be studied alongside Matthew's version of the divorce teaching and its context.
