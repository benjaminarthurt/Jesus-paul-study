## Research question

What roles do women occupy in accounts of Jesus' ministry and Paul's early Christian networks, and how are restrictions on speech and teaching to be interpreted?

## Initial observations

Luke records women supporting Jesus' ministry. Romans 16 names multiple women in Paul's networks. Interpretation of 1 Corinthians 14:34–35 involves textual discussion; 1 Timothy's Pauline authorship is debated.

## Questions to investigate

- What can participation in a ministry tell us about formal authority?
- How should the evidence from Romans 16 interact with the instruction in 1 Corinthians 14?
- What difference does classifying the Pastoral Epistles separately make?
- How do modern churches distinguish teaching, preaching, and governing?
