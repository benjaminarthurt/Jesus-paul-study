## Research question

How do Jesus' sayings about the Law compare with Paul's understanding of Torah, faith, and the position of Gentile believers?

## Initial observations

Matthew 5:17–20 and Galatians 3:23–25 are often compared but occur in very different literary and argumentative settings. Mark 7 and Romans 7 complicate a simple opposition.

## Questions to investigate

- What does "fulfill" mean in Matthew's context?
- Is Paul addressing Jewish observance, Gentile obligation, or both?
- How do the Jerusalem meeting accounts in Acts 15 and Galatians 2 compare?
- What distinction, if any, exists between covenant membership and moral obligation?

## Counterreadings required

Include readings that find continuity through Christ and readings that find a substantial shift in the Law's normative role. No final conclusion is assigned.
