## Modern question

What, if anything, do the teachings attributed to Jesus and Paul imply about concentrated wealth, poverty, charitable obligation, and social systems?

## Method

First document the ancient texts, social relations, and intended audiences. Then distinguish personal ethical duties from proposals about contemporary tax law, economic institutions, or state policy.

## Competing applications

Evaluate interpretations from multiple Christian traditions, including those emphasizing voluntary charity, institutional aid, structural reform, and cautions about overextending ancient texts.

**Research state:** topic outline, not a policy conclusion.
