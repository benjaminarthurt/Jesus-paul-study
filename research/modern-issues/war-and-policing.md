## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus' enemy-love instructions and Paul's rejection of personal revenge provide common ground. Romans 13 describes punitive public authority without explicitly assigning that role to disciples.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/enemy-love-and-civil-authority/) for passage references, alternative readings and evidence limitations.

## Modern application and boundaries

Pacifist and just-war approaches differ on whether public and personal obligations can be separated. Modern policing and military institutions require additional historical and ethical reasoning.
