## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus anticipates hostility and blesses persecuted followers; Paul connects suffering with hope and urges nonretaliation.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/suffering-and-persecution/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Disagreement, criticism, discrimination and violent persecution should not be treated as interchangeable. Religious freedom protects people across traditions and requires independent legal analysis.
