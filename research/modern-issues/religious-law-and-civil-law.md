## Ancient textual evidence

Matthew emphasizes the Law and fulfillment; Paul disputes imposing circumcision and works of the Law in Gentile inclusion and justification.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/law-and-faith/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Religious obligations within a community are not automatically enforceable civil duties. Modern legislation requires independent constitutional, ethical and empirical reasoning.
