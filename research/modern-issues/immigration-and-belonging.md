## Ancient textual evidence

Matthew depicts an initially Israel-focused mission followed by a commission to all nations. Galatians describes Gentile inclusion and disputes over shared community life.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/mission-and-gentile-inclusion/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Religious membership and citizenship are distinct institutions. Equal dignity and hospitality may inform immigration ethics, but these passages do not prescribe border policy, asylum procedures or admission quotas.
