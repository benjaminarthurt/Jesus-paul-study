## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus urges forgiveness and also describes community correction; Paul combines discipline with restoration.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/forgiveness-and-restoration/) for passage references, alternative readings and evidence limitations.

## Modern application and boundaries

Forgiveness, reconciliation, restored trust and legal accountability are distinct. The texts do not require exposing vulnerable people to further harm.
