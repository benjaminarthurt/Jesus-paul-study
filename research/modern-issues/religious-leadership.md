## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus rejects status-seeking and domination; Paul recognizes leaders and exercises disciplinary authority.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/leadership-and-authority/) for passage references, alternative readings and evidence limitations.

## Modern application and boundaries

Modern governance requires safeguards against coercion, transparent processes and independent accountability. Ancient titles cannot automatically establish modern office structures.
