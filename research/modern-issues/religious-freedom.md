## Ancient textual evidence

Gospel and Pauline texts combine Christ-centered claims with inclusion across social and ethnic boundaries.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/religious-exclusivity/) for passage references, alternative readings and evidence limitations.

## Modern application and boundaries

Theological exclusivity does not itself settle constitutional rights, freedom of conscience or the legitimacy of coercion. Interfaith cooperation and doctrinal agreement are distinct.
