## Ancient textual evidence

Gospel narratives depict healing and delegated authority; Paul describes diverse spiritual gifts and emphasizes love.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/spiritual-gifts-and-miracles/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Prayer and pastoral support can coexist with evidence-based healthcare. Ancient miracle accounts do not establish guaranteed cures or justify withholding treatment.

## Verification needs

Distinguish narrative claims, theological interpretations and independently testable clinical outcomes. Independent scholarly and policy review remains outstanding.
