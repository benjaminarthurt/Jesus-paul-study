## Ancient textual evidence

Gospel divorce restrictions and Paul's mixed-belief marriage guidance address fidelity and separation in ancient settings.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/marriage-and-divorce/) for passage references, alternative readings and evidence limitations.

## Modern application and boundaries

The texts do not supply a complete abuse-response policy. Safety, civil protection, consent and legal obligations must be evaluated separately from disputed remarriage doctrines.
