## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus challenges attachment to wealth and depicts obligations toward vulnerable people. Paul organizes collections and discusses voluntary giving.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/wealth-and-generosity/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Private generosity, mutual aid, taxation and public welfare are different mechanisms. The ancient texts provide ethical considerations but do not establish a specific tax rate or welfare model.
