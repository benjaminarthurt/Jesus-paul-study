## Ancient textual evidence

Matthew addresses adultery and desire; Paul addresses bodily union and specific misconduct in Corinth.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/sexual-ethics/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Consent, exploitation, orientation and relationship structures are distinct modern questions. Ancient terminology must not be assumed to correspond exactly to contemporary categories.
