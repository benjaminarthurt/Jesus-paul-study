## Modern question

What can ancient Gospel narratives and Pauline letters reasonably establish about women serving as pastors, preachers, elders, and teachers today?

## Method

Distinguish descriptions of participation from instructions for assemblies, debated authorship from undisputed texts, and first-century roles from later church offices.

**Research state:** topic outline, not a denominational verdict.
