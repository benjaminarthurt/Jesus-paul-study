## Ancient textual evidence

Jesus reverses status rankings; Paul addresses enslaved people within existing institutions and urges brotherly reception in Philemon. Neither selected corpus explicitly articulates a general abolition statute.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/slavery-and-social-status/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Modern forced labor and trafficking must be evaluated under contemporary human rights and legal frameworks. Ancient accommodation cannot establish moral legitimacy.

## Verification needs

Examine Roman slavery, manumission, historical reception and the distinction between theological equality and legal emancipation. Independent scholarly and policy review remains outstanding.
