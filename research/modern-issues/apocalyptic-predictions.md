## Ancient textual evidence

Mark and Matthew combine eschatological imagery with calls to vigilance; Paul describes the coming of the Lord and unexpected timing.

See the [related textual comparison](/comparisons/end-times-expectations/) for passage references and competing readings.

## Modern application and boundaries

Date-setting and claims that particular current events fulfill prophecy require evidence beyond thematic resemblance. Long-term planning and public responsibility should not be dismissed on the basis of unverified predictions.
