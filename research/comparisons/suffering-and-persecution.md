## Textual comparison

Jesus blesses persecuted followers, warns of opposition and calls disciples to take up the cross. Paul connects suffering with hope and future glory in Romans 8 and calls for blessing persecutors in Romans 12. Philippians 2 presents Christ's humiliation and exaltation as a model.

## Competing interpretations and limitations

Shared endurance and hope are clear. Paul's more developed account of participation in Christ may extend the Gospel language. Neither corpus establishes that every instance of suffering is desirable or divinely mandated.

## Modern implications

Distinguish persecution for conviction from preventable harm, abuse and ordinary illness. Endurance language must not be used to deny safety or medical care.
