## Research question

How do Jesus and Paul connect speech and anger to ethical conduct?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus links speech and inner disposition; Paul warns against mutual hostility and urges blessing rather than retaliation.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Both use severe polemical language in some contexts; the relation between rebuke and prohibited hostility requires examination.

## Verification queue

Compare rhetorical rebuke with interpersonal insult and distinguish anger from retaliatory conduct.
