## Research question

Jesus' prohibition of oath-taking in Matthew 5 raises a question about Paul's appeals to God as witness in Galatians 1:20 and 2 Corinthians 1:23.

## Preliminary textual comparison

Both value credible speech; Paul's solemn appeals may function as assurances rather than formal judicial oaths.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

If Jesus prohibits all solemn appeals invoking God, Paul's practice presents a potential tension. If the prohibition targets manipulative oath distinctions, the practices may be compatible.

## Verification queue

Examine the Greek oath vocabulary, ancient letter conventions, and whether the appeals meet the definition of an oath.
