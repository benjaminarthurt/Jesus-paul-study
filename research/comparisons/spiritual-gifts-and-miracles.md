## Textual comparison

Luke depicts Jesus granting healing authority to disciples, narrates their inability in one case and discourages monopolizing activity in his name. John 14 speaks of works by believers. Paul describes diverse gifts, emphasizes love and regulates assembly contributions.

## Competing interpretations and limitations

Paul's communal framework may extend the Gospel mission narratives. Differences between narrated miracles, promises and instructions must be preserved. The texts do not settle whether all gifts continue in every historical period.

## Modern implications

Distinguish theological claims about healing from clinical evidence and medical decisions; avoid promises of guaranteed cures.
