## Research question

How do Jesus and Paul address anxiety and uncertainty?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus directs attention toward God's care and kingdom; Paul connects prayer, peace and hope with trust amid suffering.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Neither text guarantees immediate relief from hardship; their different theological frameworks and circumstances should be preserved.

## Verification queue

Examine pastoral application without treating anxiety disorders as evidence of deficient faith.
