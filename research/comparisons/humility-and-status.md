## Research question

How do Jesus and Paul challenge the pursuit of status?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus reverses conventional honor rankings and Paul urges humility, presenting Christ's self-emptying as a model.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Paul also asserts apostolic authority; humility need not imply abandonment of every role or claim to authority.

## Verification queue

Distinguish social honor, self-assessment and institutional authority; examine the Christ hymn's literary function.
