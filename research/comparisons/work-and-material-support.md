## Research question

How do Jesus' mission instructions and teachings on anxiety relate to Paul's counsel on work and support?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus permits mission workers to receive hospitality; Paul recognizes support for proclaimers while describing his own renunciation of certain rights.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Mission-specific instructions are not automatically universal economic rules. Paul's counsel to work may address community circumstances rather than contradict trust in God.

## Verification queue

Distinguish itinerant mission, household livelihood, voluntary support and poverty.
