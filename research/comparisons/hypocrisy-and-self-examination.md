## Research question

How do the texts address moral judgment of others and examination of oneself?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus criticizes self-righteous judgment and Paul condemns judging others while practicing similar wrongdoing.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Both traditions also contain correction of others; the target may be inconsistent or self-exempting judgment rather than all moral evaluation.
