## Question

Does the role of the Law in Matthew's portrayal of Jesus conflict with the account in Galatians?

## Case for tension

The apparently enduring significance of the Law in Matthew can seem difficult to align with Paul's account of its supervisory role in a changed era of faith.

## Possible reconciliation

The texts might differ in scope: Jesus' teaching within Matthew's Jewish setting and Paul's argument about Gentile inclusion and covenant obligation.

## What remains

Examine Matthew 5 in its whole sermon, Galatians 2–5, Paul's other treatments of Torah, historical context, and serious scholarship on both readings. Classification is provisional.
