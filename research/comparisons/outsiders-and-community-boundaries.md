## Research question

How do the texts distinguish outsiders from members of a religious community?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus' Samaritan illustration challenges restrictive neighbor categories; Paul differentiates internal discipline from judgment of outsiders and urges appropriate conduct toward outsiders.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Internal membership boundaries need not contradict neighbor-love, but their application can generate exclusion or unequal treatment.

## Verification queue

Compare the categories of neighbor, outsider, fellow believer and disciplined member without assuming they are interchangeable.
