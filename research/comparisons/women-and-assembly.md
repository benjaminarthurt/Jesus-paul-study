## Question

How do female participation, recognized coworkers, and apparent restrictions on speaking or teaching relate?

## Interpretive caution

Women's presence and material support do not by themselves settle every question about office or liturgy. Romans 16, 1 Corinthians 14 and 1 Timothy 2 must be distinguished by genre, setting, textual issues, and authorship.

## What remains

Evaluate the textual witnesses for 1 Corinthians 14:34–35 and competing readings of 1 Timothy 2, without conflating these passages into a single undisputed Pauline voice.
