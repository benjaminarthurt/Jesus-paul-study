## Textual comparison

Jesus' response to the Sadducees affirms resurrection and rejects the premise that ordinary marriage arrangements continue unchanged. John connects resurrection and life with Jesus himself. Paul develops an argument about Christ as firstfruits and the transformation of the resurrection body. The contrast between natural and spiritual bodies in 1 Corinthians 15 does not automatically mean material versus immaterial existence.

## Competing interpretations and limitations

A development of shared resurrection belief is plausible; differences in chronology and imagery may also reflect distinct traditions. Neither corpus supplies a complete map of the intermediate state.

## Modern implications

Funeral teaching, bodily resurrection and popular ideas of heaven should not be conflated.
