## Research question

How do Jesus' disputes about religious observance relate to Paul's treatment of differing convictions?

## Preliminary textual comparison

Jesus challenges particular Sabbath objections; Paul explicitly develops mutual acceptance and limits liberty where another's conscience may be harmed.

## Competing interpretation and limitations

Sabbath disputes and mixed-community food practices address different circumstances. Paul's accommodation is not necessarily equivalent to Jesus' arguments.

## Verification queue

Distinguish conscience, Torah interpretation, communal meals and the scope of permissible disagreement.
