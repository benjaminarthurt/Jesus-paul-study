## Question

Does Paul's appeal for generosity preserve, soften, or reinterpret Jesus' demands surrounding possessions?

## Initial analysis

The passages focus on overlapping but nonidentical situations. Mark portrays a direct challenge to a wealthy individual; Paul appeals to communal generosity. Neither passage should be treated as a complete economic system.

## Evidence still needed

Investigate further Jesus traditions about wealth, other passages in Paul's letters, and the social conditions of each audience. Classification is provisional.
