# Chrysostom: baptism and moral responsibility

Draft reception study. [Homily 11 on Romans](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/210211.htm), opening commentary on Romans 6:5–10, English translation inspected.

Chrysostom distinguishes the gift of forgiveness in baptism from the continuing responsibility to resist wrongdoing with divine assistance. He reads Paul's doctrinal argument and ethical exhortation as interwoven. He distinguishes present moral renewal from future resurrection.

Compare Gospel calls to repentance and Paul's account of grace. This is a later synthesis, not proof that all ancient interpreters agreed. Compare Augustine's grace reception study without equating their theological systems.
