# Augsburg Confession: civil authority and oaths

Draft historical reception study. [Article XVI](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/of-civil-affairs/), English text inspected in full.

The confession permits Christians to hold civil offices, administer punishment, participate in just wars and swear oaths required by magistrates. It rejects Anabaptist opposition to civil offices and distinguishes inward evangelical righteousness from abandonment of civil institutions. It explicitly cites Acts 5:29 to limit obedience when authorities require wrongdoing.

Compare Matthew 5:33–48, Romans 12–13 and Pauline divine-witness appeals. These are proposed textual comparisons; Article XVI does not explicitly cite Romans 13 in the inspected text. Its permission for official oaths offers a concrete historical reception question concerning Jesus' prohibition. Its characterization of Anabaptists must be compared with their own documents rather than accepted as a comprehensive description.
