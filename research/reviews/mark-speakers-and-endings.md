# Who speaks at the end of Mark?

A search for what Jesus says at the tomb produces different results depending on which ending is indexed. At 16:6–7 the young man addresses the women and refers back to a promise. He speaks about Jesus. The scene ends with the women's fearful departure at 16:8. In the longer ending the narrator reports Jesus rebuking the eleven at 16:14. Jesus then addresses them directly about mission, belief and signs in 16:15–18. These are different speakers, scenes and textual questions. [Messenger at the tomb](/passages/mk-16-01-08/) · [Longer ending, with speaker boundaries](/passages/mk-16-09-20/)

WEBP prints the longer ending and explains its editors' preference in a note. Its note on 16:8 also describes an alternative brief conclusion. That additional conclusion must be distinguished from the form that simply stops at verse 8. The website displays the longer ending's teaching inventory with its textual caution, outside the core Mark sayings index. [WEBP chapter and edition notes](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK16.htm)

Houghton reports that the critical edition ECM Mark ends its reconstructed text at 16:8. His study also asks whether two medieval commentary manuscripts that break off within the verse can be counted as witnesses to that ending. He argues that their physical and commentary contexts undermine that inference. A manuscript that breaks off and an edition that intentionally concludes there are different pieces of evidence. This audit inspected Houghton's opening discussion and the English translation's notes. It did not inspect those manuscripts or the ECM apparatus. ## Similar boundaries elsewhere in the book

- At 3:28–29 Jesus warns about blasphemy against the Spirit. Verse 30 returns to the narrator, who repeats the accusers' explanation of his power. [Mark 3:20–30](/passages/mk-03-20-30/)
- At 13:14 an appeal to the reader interrupts the discourse addressed to the disciples. A verse-level quotation that ignores the parenthesis silently changes the audience. [Mark 13:14–23](/passages/mk-13-14-23/)
- At 14:58 witnesses attribute a temple saying to Jesus. The narrator characterizes their testimony as false. At 14:61–62 the high priest asks the question and Jesus gives the answer. [Mark 14:43–65](/passages/mk-14-43-65/)
- At 14:72 the narrator quotes Jesus through Peter's recollection. This supplies evidence for the narrative role of the earlier prediction, without adding a second occasion on which Jesus delivers it. [Prediction](/passages/mk-14-26-31/) · [Recollection](/passages/mk-14-66-72/)

All 81 indexed Mark parents now have a first English-text disposition. This includes the composite Mark 7 anchor, unquoted teaching, scenes with other speakers, and both ending units. The published verse spans locate evidence; the notes identify interruptions and changes of speaker within a verse. Greek collation, complete parallel mapping and independent review remain open.
