## Observation

The shared statement cites Romans 3:28 against justification through law observance while preserving commandments as standards for those justified, including Jesus' example. It distinguishes the traditions' explanatory frameworks.

## Comparative analysis

Compare Augsburg IV, Trent VI, Matthew 5 and Galatians 3. The agreement is limited to the described consensus, not universal denominational agreement. The retrieved Vatican text is the 1997 document; final 1999 signing materials require separate inspection.
