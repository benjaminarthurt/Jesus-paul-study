## Observation

The confession distinguishes the state's coercive function from Christian discipleship, rejecting Christian participation in armed force and magistracy. Its argument combines Gospel examples with Pauline conformity to Christ (Romans 8:29). Its use of the adulterous woman requires a textual-critical caveat concerning John 7:53–8:11.

## Comparative analysis

Compare Augsburg XVI and Aquinas II-II q.40. The difference is an interpretive conflict between later communities, not proof that Paul and Jesus directly contradict each other.
