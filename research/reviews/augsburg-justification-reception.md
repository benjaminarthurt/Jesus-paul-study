# Augsburg Confession: justification (1530)

Draft historical reception study. [Article IV](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/of-justification/), English text inspected in full.

The confession explicitly cites Romans 3–4 in grounding justification in Christ and faith rather than human merit. This is direct documentary evidence of Pauline reception in a Lutheran confessional standard. Article IV alone does not establish that good works are irrelevant to Christian life; Articles VI and XX require separate inspection.

Compare Matthew 25 and Romans 2 with the project's salvation-and-judgment study. This comparison is a research question, not evidence that the confession explicitly rejects Matthew. Compare Trent Session VI, which also denies that antecedent works earn initial justification but differs on the function of grace and cooperation.
