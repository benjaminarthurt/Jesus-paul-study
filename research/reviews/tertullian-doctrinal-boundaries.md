# Tertullian: doctrinal boundaries and apostolic authority

Draft reception study. [Prescription Against Heretics](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0311.htm), chapters 1–4, English translation excerpts inspected.

Chapter 1 invokes 1 Corinthians 11:19 in treating doctrinal divisions as tests of faith. Chapter 3 combines Gospel warnings about perseverance and apostasy with material from the disputed Pauline Pastorals. Chapter 4 expressly coordinates the Lord's teachings with apostolic correspondence, interpreting Matthew 7:15 as a warning against deceptive doctrinal opponents.

This is evidence of a later author using Gospel and Pauline traditions together to justify doctrinal boundaries. It does not establish unanimity among ancient churches or equivalence between Jesus' warnings and later institutions' enforcement mechanisms.
