# Speech, retaliation and Jesus tradition in Romans 12

Retain draft agreement between the Gospel warnings about harmful speech and Paul's rejection of retaliatory conduct.

## Checks
- Romans 12:14 explicitly replaces cursing persecutors with blessing. Verses 17–21 extend the ethic beyond speech into action.
- Matthew 5 and Luke 6 supply parallels, but Paul does not explicitly attribute Romans 12:14 to Jesus.
- The 2024 article Love’s Limits in Paul of Tarsus and Seneca the Younger identifies probable Synoptic-tradition echoes while noting scholarly doubts about dependence.
- Paffenroth's 1992 discussion favors a relationship with the tradition reflected in Luke while cautioning against overstatement of its importance to Paul.
- Galatians 5 describes destructive conflict within communities; this is a related but distinct setting from persecution.
- Paul's own polemical language warrants separate investigation before claiming uniform application of his ethical advice.
