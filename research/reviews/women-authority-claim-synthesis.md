## Editorial rules for eventual website

1. Distinguish **directly attributed speech**, **narrator description**, **Paul's first-person claims**, and **disputed Pauline attribution** in visible labels.
2. Show evidence **for and against** a proposed conflict; never treat an unresolved manuscript question as a settled fact.
3. Mark claims with uncertain Greek semantics or missing full-text scholarship as **draft**.
4. Present contemporary denominational interpretations as named positions with official citations, not as objective historical findings.
5. Avoid using the modern category “ordination” as a translation of ancient role terms unless a historical bridge is documented.
