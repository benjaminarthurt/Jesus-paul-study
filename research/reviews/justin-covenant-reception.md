# Justin Martyr: covenant and religious boundaries

Draft reception study. [Dialogue with Trypho](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/01282.htm), chapters 10–13, English translation inspected.

Chapter 10 stages a dispute over circumcision and festivals. Chapter 11 argues that Christ inaugurates a universal covenant replacing the earlier one, appealing to Isaiah, Jeremiah and Abraham. Chapter 12 reinterprets Sabbath observance in moral terms. Chapter 13 associates forgiveness with repentance and Christ's death.

Compare Matthew 5:17–20, Galatians 3 and Romans 14. Justin's explicit replacement framework warrants comparison with Paul's covenant arguments but cannot be attributed exclusively to Paul: Justin invokes Israel's scriptures directly. The dialogue is a literary representation, not a transcript of Jewish opinion. Its anti-Jewish polemic must be analyzed rather than adopted.
