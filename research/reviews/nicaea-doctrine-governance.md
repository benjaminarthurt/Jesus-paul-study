# Nicaea (325): doctrine and institutional governance

Draft reception study. [Creed and canons of the First Council of Nicaea](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm), creed and canons 2–5, 8–11, English translation inspected.

The creed asserts the Son's consubstantiality with the Father and excludes competing formulations. Canon 2 expressly invokes the rule against appointing a recent convert from 1 Timothy 3:6, a disputed Pauline letter in the project's authorship framework. Canon 4 regulates episcopal appointment. Canon 5 establishes provincial review of exclusions, including examination of whether a bishop acted improperly. Canon 8 conditions reintegration of Cathari clergy on doctrinal and disciplinary agreement. Canon 11 provides a penitential restoration process.

Compare leadership, identity and divinity, and forgiveness studies. Canon 2 provides direct evidence of reception of a Pauline-attributed text in institutional rules. The creed's relationship to particular Gospel and Pauline passages requires further evidence. Do not infer that Nicaea determined the biblical canon or that every rule was universally implemented.
