# Aquinas: property and shared use

Draft reception study. [Summa Theologiae II-II, q. 66, aa. 1–2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/3066.htm), selected English translation inspected.

Aquinas distinguishes human administration of property from its use for common needs. Private ownership is justified by practical considerations but does not extinguish duties of sharing. Article 2 explicitly invokes 1 Timothy 6:17–18 (disputed Pauline authorship in this project). Article 1 addresses Basil's interpretation of Luke 12:18 and challenges the rich man's assumption of absolute ownership.

Compare Gospel wealth teachings and Pauline collection practices. This synthesis draws on natural-law reasoning, patristic commentary and scripture, not exclusively Paul. Neither modern capitalism nor socialism follows directly from it.
