## Observation

The confession limits baptism to persons professing repentance and faith and connects the practice to participation in Christ's death and resurrection.

## Comparative analysis

Compare Matthew 28 and Romans 6; the latter is a thematic comparison unless explicit dependence is established. Its rejection of infant baptism is a later confessional position.
