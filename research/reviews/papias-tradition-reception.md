# Papias: transmission of Jesus traditions

## Primary evidence and source layers

[Fragments of Papias](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0125.htm), translated by Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson, Ante-Nicene Fathers 1 (1885), fragments I and VI, retrieved English excerpts. The surviving testimony is mediated through later writers; it is not an intact copy of Papias's work.

Fragment I presents Papias as seeking reports from people associated with elders and preferring oral testimony. Fragment VI preserves reported traditions about Mark and Matthew through Eusebius. The identity of John, the relationship of the writings described to canonical Gospels, and the accuracy of the transmission claims require independent assessment.

## Comparative significance

The testimony supports investigation of channels of Jesus tradition beyond Pauline letters. It does not demonstrate that Paul was rejected by Papias or absent from his lost work. Fragment V in the compilation contains Pauline material, but attribution must be investigated independently before treating it as Papias's own words.
