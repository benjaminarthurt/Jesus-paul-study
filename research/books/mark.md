# Gospel of Mark — first inventory pass

**Status:** draft. A structured set of 77 new preliminary literary units from Mark's 16 chapters; not a fully verified word-by-word inventory of Jesus' sayings.

## Primary source

World English Bible Updated (66-book edition): [Mark chapter index](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK.htm). Each passage record in `research/index/passages.json` includes a link to the source chapter. Original summaries are paraphrases, not biblical quotations.

## What is indexed?

Direct recorded instruction, dialogue, parables, narrated action, and contextual passages. A narrated event may be essential to Mark's portrayal of Jesus without being a teaching he is reported to have spoken. The legacy Mark 7:1–23 entry remains as a composite reference for existing research.

## Selected textual and interpretive cautions

- **Mark 16:9–20:** The longer ending appears in many witnesses, but is absent from some early Greek manuscripts and widely judged secondary in modern textual criticism. Alternative arguments for its authenticity also exist. Do **not** treat the commission and signs there as an uncontested statement in the original Markan text. See [WEB note and text](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK16.htm), [NET Bible note](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%2016%3A9-20&version=NET).
- **Mark 7:19:** Whether the phrase about clean foods is represented as Jesus' speech or the narrator's explanation matters for theology; inspect translation choices and Greek syntax.
- **Mark 9:29, 9:44, 9:46; 11:26:** Wording and/or inclusion differs in textual traditions and should be checked against a scholarly critical apparatus before quoting.
- **Mark 12:41–44:** A widow's gift is sometimes read as praise and sometimes against nearby denunciations of exploitation; further investigation is necessary.
